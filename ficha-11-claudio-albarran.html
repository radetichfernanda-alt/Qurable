Claudio Albarrán
Chile, Audiovisual Producer
“This is a great window.”

I studied music for a couple of years before beginning my audiovisual career. So sound has always been present in my creative space. But it wasn’t until I started using digital and virtual sound production software that I really realized the power of it, of digital sound.

I have used atmospheric sound to set the scene for different multimedia displays of my photographic works. Regarding the image, I have worked in recent years only in digital format, trying to transfer the link with the organic, to the data, building an ambiguous visuality between both aesthetics, leaving aside the variant of the photographic error and substituting it for control over the photographic image, through different digital post-production processes. You can access information that seems like an “analog error”, but everything is actually controlled thanks to digital tools.
