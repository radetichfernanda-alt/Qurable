Jazmin Grinbaum
Argentina, Architect & Visual Artist
“The metaverse is kind of science fiction. It is an imminent new dimension which one can embrace or not. I’m interested in my work being translated to different languages and circling both universes.”

I’m an architect. In fact, I entered the world of visual arts through architecture. I did a master’s degree in Conceptual Design that had a lot to do with the mix between these two universes. Through that degree and my friends, professors and teachers, is that I got to work in projects that had to do with the design of samples, art fairs or spaces for art, while simultaneously producing my own work.

Coming from the world of architecture, I’m very tied to spatiality. I’m very interested in the implications of inhabiting a space and the exploration of its materials. Many elements coming from architecture, especially the intimate space of home, strain through my works and become something else: utilitarian objects such as curtains or benches become a hybrid. I like to think that a lot of what I do can be linked to the architecture of homes, from the objectual to the emotional.
