Angel Gabriel
Argentina, Visual Artist
“Digital platforms are another tool out of the set. The work I’m doing now will probably take me somewhere else, as I’m exploring new stuff; that new place is unknown and a new road to travel.”

I consider my work to be fundamentally conceptual. I’m interested in seduction and sensuality, whether it’s manifested through drawings, paintings, sculptures, digital works, videos or installations related to ornaments. I’m not interested in setting a specific time and space because my work is open to infinity, regardless of being trapped in an image. The concept of structure, thought of as a force holding elements together cohesively, regulating and ordering them as well, is key to my work. It surges involuntarily, from the direct observation of nature. I find in it’s form a primary beauty which, after a process of dispossession, I can reduce to a minimal unit and, through repetition and combination, use it to create new forms. Even though the process may seem mechanical, it's in fact unlimited, with an infinite amount of possible roads.

I’m still not able to think of how conceptual or formal ways of work and understanding the world could run out of possibilities. I’m certainly not interested in sticking to a particular technique.
