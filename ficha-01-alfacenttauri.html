Alfacenttauri
Chile, Multidisciplinary Artist
“My proposal and research move toward building a material-creative semiotics, where the narrative behind the creative margin is transparent to contemporary discussions.”

My work centers on aesthetic processes related to technology and the exploration of the internet as the main environment for sensitive interactions. 3D manipulation and digital processes are devices for exploring a fantastical, utopian narrative through digital ecosystems, polymorphic creatures, and rhizomatic entities, with the aim of investigating the relationship between a corrupted nature and a subjectivity in crisis, from a third-world perspective.

In other words, I allow myself to create my own fabulation that addresses these correlations, to build a narrative and a world that can project discursive and contextual dislocations, which touch on and pervade the contemporary world, where (the end of the world, extinction, and cataclysm) are presented through dialectical and corrosive means.
