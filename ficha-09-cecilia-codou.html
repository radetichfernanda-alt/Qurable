Cecilia Codou
Chile, Visual Artist
“Possibilities appear for new artists and unexplored creative processes in the field of contemporary art, or maybe the have actually been explored only there is not a market for them yet.”

My work is based in the recopilation of files, not only digital but also physical images. I start my creative process through the search for social problems, which usually derive on to personal analysis, taking into account the contemporary frame in which we are situated.

I collect files that I later edit and relate to form new stories that propose a critique and social questioning and even sometimes possible solutions or ideas to work on such problematic scenarios.

My creative process has varied in relation to strategies of creation. I’ve worked on images from their photographic shot and transformation, experimenting with techniques such as digital collage from the workshop work to the creative processes involving external participation. That’s why I nowadays combine the workshop work with field work, I’m interested in having the process hold a communication with others or communities: in those cases I don’t set the problems before hand, as they surge from questions and variation from the original topic.

What’s interesting about these creative processes is that they don’t aim for visuals to be a unique, successful result, but to have the experience be the most important aspect, the most enriching aspect of this work is, in the end, the connections it allows with others and the collective questions that generates, getting the art to show through the process itself. A final product is not needed.

There are many ways to do art. The field is too wide. It’s not only about whether the artwork is understandable or not. Someone connecting with the work enthusiastically is enough for the work to be a success. Artworks can be understood by others from a different perspective which may be as interesting as the message that the artist is trying to convey, people, spectators can complement the original tale.
