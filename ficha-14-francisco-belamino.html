Francisco Belamino
Chile, Visual Artist
“I’m interested in thinking about the collection of images as a narrative, with the challenge implied in displaying the works in a web platform. Seeing and knowing, even forming communities with other artists, I find that super interesting as well. The new generations make scenes of art through their links in the web, that is an intangible value.”

In my works, I reflect upon the idea of the screen as a matriz of thought that indoctrinated the sight. Through screens we look at images of the world: a kind of condition to see, that is basically its surface. The first works I did were experimental: I took monitors, broke them and left their circuits in sight. Then I focused on getting to the most infimum point in the screen, the RGB matter.

I understand images in movements as something concrete and physical. For example, the cinema has always been thought of as a window to the world. Nevertheless, it has always been a mere wall, opaque, plane and robust. I’m interested in experimenting with the physical notions as well as the digital, and how to trespass one and the other, to finally find what is the relation between the material support and the image.

The image is a framing, and its materials are the condition to its existence. It is not the same to see images in a little or a big screen, the quality, the resolution, etc. In the use of technology, there is an ideological and political factor that strains through different spheres of my work.
