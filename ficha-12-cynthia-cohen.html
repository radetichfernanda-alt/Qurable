Cynthia Cohen
Argentina, Painter
“I’m feeding on both universes: the digital and that of paint. I come and I go. In my creative field, those worlds begin to play. The language of one allows me to act on the other.”

On a creative level, for a painter like myself, digital tools imply an infinite multiplication of the ideas and images of my universe. I find fascinating the endless possibilities of what can be generated through digital matters, stemming from the production of spontaneous situations and immediate pictures of it. With the timing required for painting, that would be impossible.

Even though I’m ruled by paint, I’m nowadays exploring more of a spatial work, a sort of sculpturistic installation. But, since I became interested in NFTs, I found an opportunity in relation to my compositions that I had not found in paint: digital production allowed me a more spontaneous situation with a level that would take months in painting, and that fascinates me.
