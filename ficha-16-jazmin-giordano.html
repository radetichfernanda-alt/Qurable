Jazmin Giordano
Argentina, Visual Artist
“I’m interested in the relation between paint and other media, mainly video.”

I paint since I was a child. I went to art school for workshops and college. For a long time, I only traditionally painted because it hadn’t crossed my mind that I could do something else until I started attending meetings where art works were analyzed. There I started experimenting with paint in relation with other disciplines, and it was that way that I got to videos. Nowadays the main forms of expression that I use are paint and video art, and maybe a mix of both, such as my installation.

I’m a person that gets bored of a single technique or of being in a single project for a long time, hence this coming and going between traditional practice and the digital, more experimental techniques, which even allow me to incorporate another façade, which is performance, came to me as the best way of working.

Conceptually, I’m interested in spaces where traditional archetypes are diffused, as well as ordinary places which traverse Christian and Occidental cultures. I like transiting through spaces I had never explored before which stand differently, yet open and plural.

My work stems from the association through intimacy of a pluriverse of symbols that meet and are repeated and recycled from a work to another and from a series to the next one.

During 2017 and 2018, taking as a creative resource the crossing between the communication strategies in the elective campaign of Argentina with business or spiritual coaching speeches, I started a video art project called “Salto de fe” (Leap of faith). This project, which started as a proposal for a physical experience, has now its own Instagram account not only for exposition but for conceptual content.
