Carlos Orrego
Chile, Software Engineer, Entrepreneur
“I think NFTs allow visual artists to finance their work without intermediaries or the need to take their art to analogical formats. Plus, it makes you part of a worldwide network of artists and new technology enthusiasts, allowing you to enter, feed and be part of the metaverse.”

I am a programmer and software developer. That is my day to day.

I come from a family of artists, I have those roots and thanks to that I got started in the world of art. I did my master’s in Computer Science at the prestigious lab of Electronic Visualization at UIC. At that time, I was very interested in developing games. I studied in Chicago, where technology was at a peak during the years of ‘96, ‘97, ‘98. There was already virtual reality. There I started working with technology, collaborating with many artists and encountering the digital arts of those times.

In my case, the visual code part is quite easy. First, I started investigating entropy, the concept that speaks of us as energy and of life as the construction that we ourselves create. I’m fascinated by all things related to thermodynamics, entropic energy. There on, I started messing with various different algorithms of visualization and generating chaotic data.

Data is the trail left behind by a moment that passed in the physical and sensory world. In my work, it is visible how the data of the physical world repercutes in virtual/visual works. In that sense, we are nowadays in a historic moment bridging the digital and the analogical.
