Diego PerMar
Mexico, Multidisciplinary Artist
“I really like to invoke a certain level of nostalgia with my art, and adding lore to my work so viewers can let their imagination fly and piece together a story of their own.”

Born in San Luis Potosí, Mexico to a family of storytellers, he studied abroad in the United States for 5 years, then returned to México and learned to appreciate his roots and cultural heritage. This is represented in his artworks with vibrant colors that mirror the work of Mexican artisans, combined with technology, fantasy, and storytelling.

I start my creative process by listing my favorite things in life (cats, fantasy, iridescence, colorful things, candy, computers, etc), things that keep me going. Then I start sketching artworks I would like to make. I always procure that people who experience my work take away a moment of escapism, and I like to add lore to my work so viewers can let their imagination fly and piece together a story of their own.

NFT tech has the unique ability to bring some sense of tangibility to a rather intangible future where every single aspect of our life is constantly digitalized. It is important to have a beacon of light that tells everybody what is real and what is not.
