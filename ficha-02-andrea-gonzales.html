Andrea Gonzales
Chile, Visual Artist, Photographer and Videographer
“This platform and new system of collection are interesting. I like to think about the selection of pieces through a certain criteria, not just a file out of the many in our computer, but something that gives meaning.”

There’s something in the aesthetic of my work that perpetuates, maybe unconsciously, a view of estrangement that reveals an uncertain outlook, where what is going to happen is unknown or misunderstood.

Lately when I begin working, I don’t start from a preconceived idea or rationalization about something, but from impulse, intuition and curiosity. I’m motivated by the idea of collecting files that I download from the internet: writings, pictures, videos, to then edit and work with, creating something from that confluence. I believe its during that process that my work surges. At a certain moment, I know my work is done, and I can understand then what it was about, or where it leads.

If I reflect upon the take-in of my pieces, I realize they don’t make immediate impact, no euphoria or amazement, it holds a different rhythm, a different way of seeing it. I see people observe my work and perceive a reaction of introspection and stillness, as if they needed time to process what they see.
