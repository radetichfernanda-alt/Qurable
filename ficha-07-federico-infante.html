Federico Infante
Chile, Visual Artist
“A new window has opened to communicate my work and, in this way, invite more people to participate, who may not be related with the world of art or who hadn’t had the opportunity to consider themselves collectionists, it is a unique opportunity.”

I came to New York in 2010 to study on the MFA school of visual arts. This gave me the possibility of taking two years to experiment and explore new tools for my work. It was a very productive period, filled with opportunities. It opened doors to the world of New York’s art and allowed me to build relations with collectionists and galleries.

Nowadays I find myself in a quite similar process, aiming to experiment and open to new possibilities which offer new media to manipulate my images. My digital works have an analogical beginning, a base of paint or an original drawing. The digital process of animation that I give to the image is similar to the work that I do in painting layers, textures and backgrounds. The image starts to form almost organically due to the interactions between these elements.

In this context I started to become interested in the opportunity to tell a story, building narratives that can give movement to my works. In my mind, the paintings move inside an atmosphere that throbs, and that is why I feel comfortable working with animation for it allows me to bring to life visually that movement I imagine. I want to keep exploring with these tools and my works to show how far I get without missing the balance between the analogical and the digital.
